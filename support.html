“Compose Email” opens a new message in your phone or computer’s email app with our address already filled in. This works on iPhone, iPad, and most computers that have an email app already set up.

  • What kind of phone or tablet you’re using (iPhone or Android), if it’s relevant.
  • A short description of what’s happening — and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot if you can. It often saves a round of back-and-forth.

Common Questions

Is RxOrderly free?

Yes. The core features — tracking up to 5 medications, dose reminders, refill alerts, and your Medical ID — are free forever, with no credit card required. An optional Premium upgrade unlocks unlimited medications and additional features.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your medication and health information stays on your device by default. We never sell your data, we show no ads, and we take no money from pharmaceutical companies — so we have no incentive to push you toward any particular medication or pharmacy. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

My reminder didn’t go off — what happened?

Reminders depend on your device’s notification settings, the operating system, and connectivity. If a reminder doesn’t appear when you expected it, check:

  • RxOrderly has notification permission (your phone’s Settings › Notifications › RxOrderly).
  • Do Not Disturb or Focus mode isn’t blocking it.
  • The phone isn’t in silent or battery-saver mode at the reminder time.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Premium subscriptions are managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by us. To cancel, open the Subscriptions screen in your store’s settings on your device. Rivka SVCS LLC cannot see, charge, or refund your subscription directly — only the store can. If you need help finding the cancel button on your device, write us and we’ll walk you through it.

Can a caregiver or family member access my information?

Not currently. RxOrderly is single-user per device today. Multi-user and caregiver features are planned for a future tier.

I lost my phone — is my data gone?

On the free plan, RxOrderly stores your medication data on your device only, so if the phone is lost the data is lost with it. A future Premium tier will offer optional cloud backup so this isn’t an issue. Until then, we recommend keeping your medication list in a second place — a written list at home or with a family member — for peace of mind.
