Privacy Policy

Effective: May 28, 2026  ·  Rivka SVCS LLC  ·  Los Angeles, CA

Your medication data stays on your device by default. In the Free tier, RxOrderly does not transmit your personal health information off your phone, and we do not sell your data. A future Premium tier will offer optional, opt-in cloud backup, with full disclosure before any data is uploaded — see Section 1.

Overview

RxOrderly is a medication management app built for privacy. Your medication data lives on your device and is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the app.

How RxOrderly Is Funded

RxOrderly works for you, not for the drug industry. We take no money from pharmaceutical companies. We show no ads. We never sell your health information. We never recommend or push a particular medication — RxOrderly only helps you keep track of what you and your doctor have already decided. And we earn nothing from your prescriptions or the pharmacies you use. RxOrderly is funded by your subscription, period.

The one exception is supplements. If you choose to buy a vitamin or supplement through a link we offer in the app, we may earn a small commission from the retailer (for example, iHerb or Amazon Associates). This applies only to supplements — never to your prescription medications or pharmacies — and it never changes the prices or options we show you. Where such a link appears in the app, we identify the commercial relationship clearly at the link itself.

1. Information We Collect

Your medication data (Free tier)
All medication names, dosages, schedules, refill history, doctor visit notes, and supply counts are stored exclusively on your device using a local database. This data is encrypted at rest by your device’s operating system, Rivka SVCS LLC has no access to it, and you are the only person who can view it. If you contact us for support and choose to share data with us — for example, by exporting and sending a database file — we will use that data solely to assist you and will not retain it afterward.

Anonymous usage analytics (all tiers)
We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate — which screens are visited, which features are used, and where users encounter friction. This data is anonymous, cannot be traced back to you, and contains no medication information.

Crash reports (all tiers)
We use Sentry to receive automatic crash reports when the app encounters an error. Crash reports include device type, operating system version, and a technical stack trace describing what the app was doing at the time of the crash. They do not include your name, your medications, or any health information.

Location (when you use local resource features)
When you use app features that help you find local resources — such as nearby pharmacies or savings programs — RxOrderly requests access to your device location. This is used solely to return nearby results. Your location is not stored by Rivka SVCS LLC.

Premium tier — cloud backup (coming soon)
A future Premium tier will offer optional cloud backup, multi-device sync, and account-based features powered by a secure third-party cloud service. When this feature launches, it will require creating an account (email address). Medication data synced to the cloud will be encrypted in transit and at rest. This section will be updated before that feature is released.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name or email address in the Free tier. We do not share any data with insurance companies, pharmaceutical companies, advertisers, or data brokers. We never sell your data.

4. Refill Requests & Pharmacy Communication

When you request a refill or contact a prescriber or pharmacy through RxOrderly, the app composes a message — an email or text — using your own email or messaging app. The message may contain the medication name, dosage, prescription number (if available), your contact information, and the pharmacy or prescriber you have selected. The message is sent from your own device through your own email or text app, directly to the recipient you choose. Rivka SVCS LLC does not transmit, intercept, store, or have access to the contents of these messages.

The pharmacy or prescriber who receives your refill request handles your information under their own privacy practices, which are not controlled by us. You should review the privacy policy of any pharmacy or prescriber with whom you communicate.

5. Subscriptions & Billing

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. Rivka SVCS LLC does not receive or store your payment information.

6. Children

RxOrderly is intended for adults aged 18 or older managing their own medications and is not designed for use by minors. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

7. California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected about them, to request deletion of that information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, contact us at support@rxorderly.com.

8. Data Retention

Medication data stored on your device is retained until you delete the app or manually clear it within the app. Anonymous analytics and crash report data is retained per the policies of Google Firebase and Sentry respectively.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update these policies in the future. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Material changes will be noted in the app release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at support@rxorderly.com.
